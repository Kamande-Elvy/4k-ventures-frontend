Where our clients are headed

Visas, flights and visiting us

ApprovalCan you guarantee my visa?

No, and nobody honest can. Only the embassy or immigration authority decides. What we do is make sure your application is complete, accurate and well prepared, which is the part you control.

Anyone who promises approval, or says they have contacts at an embassy, is not telling you the truth. Read our visa disclaimer.

DocumentsWhat documents do I need?

It depends on where you are going and why. The document checker gives you a starting list for UAE and US visas, and we confirm your exact list at your consultation.

TimingHow long does it take?

It depends on the destination, the visa type and how busy the embassy is. US visas need an interview, and appointment waits in Nairobi can be long, so start early. We give you a realistic timeline for your case before you pay anything.

FeesHow much do you charge?

Our service fee depends on the visa and how much help you need. We tell you the full amount, along with the government fees, before any work begins. There are no extras added later.

FlightsCan you book my flight too?

Yes. We compare options and book international flights for individuals, groups and companies. If you are still waiting on a visa, we will tell you when it is safe to buy.

Visit usWhere is your office?

Suite B18, 1st floor, Rehema House, on University Way at the corner with Koinange Street, opposite the University of Nairobi Main Campus. We are open Monday to Friday, 8:30 am to 5:30 pm.

StudentsDo you help with US student visas?

Yes. Once your school has issued your I-20, we help with the SEVIS fee, the DS-160, your financial documents and preparation for the interview.

RemoteDo I have to come to the office?

No. Many clients do everything on WhatsApp and email. You are always welcome at Suite B18 if you would rather talk face to face or hand over documents in person.

StaysCan you arrange hotels and transfers?

Yes, on request. Tell us your dates and budget and we will book rooms and airport pick-ups to fit the rest of your trip, in Kenya or abroad.

PrivacyIs my passport and personal data safe with you?

Your documents are only seen by the staff working on your application and are used only for that purpose. See our privacy policy for the detail.
