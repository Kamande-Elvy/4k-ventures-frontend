Visa Assistance

UAE / Dubai Visa Assistance

Support built for first-time and returning travellers applying for a UAE or Dubai visa -- from your first consultation through to the day you fly.

Who this service is for

Kenyan travellers applying for a UAE or Dubai visa for tourism, transit, family visits or business -- whether it's your first application or you've been through the process before and want it handled properly this time.

How the application process works

We start with a consultation to understand your trip and travel history, then guide you through document preparation, submission and follow-up, keeping you informed at every stage.

Requirements & document checklist

  • Valid passport with sufficient validity
  • Passport-size photographs meeting UAE specifications
  • Proof of travel dates and itinerary
  • Supporting financial documentation
  • Any additional documents specific to your visa category

Exact requirements vary by applicant and visa category -- we confirm your specific checklist during consultation.

What's included

Support at every stage

Consultation

An upfront conversation about your trip, timeline and visa category.

Document review

We check your documents before submission, not after a rejection.

Application support

Hands-on help preparing and lodging your application.

Progress communication

Regular updates so you're never left wondering where things stand.

Travel planning

Guidance on timing your trip around your visa approval.

Flight booking

Optional flight quotations and booking once your visa is in hand.

Visa approval is granted solely at the discretion of the relevant embassy or consulate. 4KS Ventures provides application support and guidance; we do not guarantee visa approval.
