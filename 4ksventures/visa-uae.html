How we help

Who this is for

Kenyan travellers applying for a UAE or Dubai visa for tourism, transit, family visits or business, whether it's your first application or you've applied before and want it handled properly this time.

How the process works

We start with a consultation about your trip and travel history, then guide you through preparing your documents, lodging the application and following it up, with an update at every stage.

What's included

  • Consultation. An upfront conversation about your trip, timeline and visa type.
  • Document review. We check everything before it's submitted, not after a refusal.
  • Application support. Hands-on help preparing and lodging your application.
  • Progress updates. You always know where your application stands.
  • Travel timing. Advice on when to book, based on your approval.
  • Flight booking. Optional quotes and booking once your visa is in hand.

We don't guarantee approval

Only the UAE immigration authorities decide whether to grant a visa. We make sure your application is complete and accurate; we cannot influence the decision, and nobody who claims they can should be trusted.

Government and visa fees are set by the authorities and are not refundable if a visa is refused. Read our full visa disclaimer.
