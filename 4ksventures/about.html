About Us

A Kenyan-origin travel house, built on plain-spoken service

4KS Ventures Tours and Travel Ltd was founded to make international travel planning and visa application support more professional, transparent and accessible for Kenyan travellers.

Our Vision

To become a trusted Kenyan-origin travel and tourism group serving international travellers and, over time, a recognised East African tourism platform.

Our Mission

To make international travel planning and visa application support more professional, transparent and accessible for Kenyan travellers.

Our motto

Professional. Transparent. Accessible.

Three words that describe exactly how we intend every client interaction to feel -- whether that's your first enquiry call or your visa interview preparation the night before.

Core values

What guides how we work

Professionalism

Every application and itinerary is handled with the same care and attention to detail, regardless of destination or budget.

Transparency

Clear pricing, honest timelines, and straight answers -- including when the honest answer is that we don't yet know.

Customer commitment

We measure success by whether you felt supported, not just by whether the paperwork got filed.
