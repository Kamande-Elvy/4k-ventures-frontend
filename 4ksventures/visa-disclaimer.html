Visa Disclaimer

4KS Ventures Tours and Travel Ltd provides professional guidance and support throughout the visa application process, including consultation, document review, application preparation and interview preparation where applicable.

No guarantee of approval

Visa approval is determined solely by the embassy, consulate or immigration authority of the destination country, based on their own criteria. 4KS Ventures does not issue visas and cannot guarantee that any application will be approved, regardless of the support provided.

Accuracy of information

Visa requirements can change without notice. We make reasonable efforts to keep our guidance current, but you should always verify critical requirements with the relevant embassy or consulate.
