Visa disclaimer

What we do, what we cannot do, and what that means for you. Please read it before you apply.

4KS Ventures Tours and Travel Ltd ("4KS Ventures", "we") is a private travel company. We provide guidance and support with visa applications, including consultation, document review, help completing forms, application preparation and interview preparation where it applies.

We are not a government agency

We are not an embassy, consulate, visa application centre or immigration authority, and we are not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them. We do not issue visas.

No guarantee of approval

Every visa decision is made solely by the embassy, consulate or immigration authority of the destination country, using its own criteria. We cannot guarantee that any application will be approved, whatever support we provide, and we cannot speed up a decision.

We cannot influence a decision

We have no special access, contacts or arrangements with any embassy or official. Anyone who claims they can secure a visa through contacts, or for an extra payment, is not telling the truth, and paying them may break the law. We will never ask you for money for that purpose.

Your information must be true

You are responsible for the accuracy and honesty of everything in your application. We will not prepare or submit false documents or misleading information, and we will stop working on an application if we believe it contains any. False information can lead to refusal and to a ban on future travel.

Fees

  • Our service fee pays for our time and guidance. It is not a fee for the visa itself and it does not change the outcome.
  • Government visa fees, appointment fees and other third-party charges are set by those bodies and are usually not refundable if a visa is refused.
  • We tell you all fees in writing before any work begins. See our refund and cancellation policy.

Requirements change

Visa rules, fees and processing times can change without notice. We keep our guidance current, but you should check critical requirements with the relevant embassy or its official website before you travel.

Book travel carefully

We advise you not to buy non-refundable flights or accommodation until your visa is approved, unless the embassy requires a confirmed booking. We are not responsible for costs you incur before a decision is made.
