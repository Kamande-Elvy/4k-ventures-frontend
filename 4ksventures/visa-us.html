Visa Assistance

U.S. Visa Assistance

The U.S. visa process asks a lot of applicants. We help you prepare thoroughly -- documents, forms and interview readiness -- so you walk in with confidence.

Application guidance

We walk you through the DS-160 and the wider application process step by step, explaining what each section is actually asking for.

Supporting-document guidance

Clear guidance on the supporting documents that strengthen your application, tailored to your visa category and travel history.

Document organisation

We help you organise your documents so everything is ready and easy to present, both for submission and at interview.

Interview preparation

Practical preparation for your consular interview -- what to expect, how to present your case clearly, and how to stay composed under questioning.

Travel planning

Once your visa is approved, we help you plan the trip itself, including flight timing.

General application support

A single point of contact throughout, so you're not piecing guidance together from forums and hearsay.

Visa approval is granted solely at the discretion of the relevant embassy or consulate. 4KS Ventures provides application support and guidance; we do not guarantee visa approval.
